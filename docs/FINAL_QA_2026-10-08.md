# Final integration QA — 2026-10-08

## Result and scope

The final local checkout passes TypeScript checking, all **426 tests in 34 files**, and the production build through `npm run check`. The test configuration now limits Vitest to two workers. An earlier unconstrained run exited unexpectedly after 418 tests; the bounded full suite and then the complete aggregate check both succeeded. The cause of the earlier process exit was not conclusively established.

This pass inspected the actual local Vite app in the cloud Chromium browser. Desktop screenshots are 1180×757. Mobile checks used an actual **320×680 iframe viewport**, not a scaled desktop image. Only one local game tab was mounted at a time, respecting the origin-wide play-session lock. Production and unrelated tabs were left untouched. All wagers below used local play credits.

The final integration also replaces the Classic lobby placeholder with the authored `ClassicSlotArt` used by its cabinet. No publication, commit, or deployment was performed by this QA pass.

## Direct browser evidence

### Classic 3-Reel Slot

- Desktop and 320×680: the burgundy/brass cabinet, painted fruit, seven and diamond, payout strip, complete three-reel board and all essential controls rendered cleanly.
- The mobile cabinet and its spin button fit inside the viewport without horizontal overflow or page scrolling.
- One manual 1-credit spin debited 788.04 to 787.04, disabled stake/mode controls during playback, landed seven/grape/lemon, displayed “No win,” and re-enabled the controls. No second debit was observed.
- The final lobby registry uses the matching painted Classic card instead of the emoji placeholder.

### Big Bass Bonanza

- Desktop and 320×680: inspected the corrected canonical fishing float, rod, dragonfly, tackle box, bass, A/K/Q/J and 10 artwork. The mobile board and all primary controls fit in the viewport.
- The rules dialog visibly lists those symbol classes and the published payout rows, including the float's two-symbol award. It also discloses local fish/extra-fish frequencies, the 2,100× cap and the local 100× demo shortcut.
- This final pass did not replay a complete Bass fishing bonus. Its batch transitions, collection math, payouts and caps are covered by the final automated suite and the separate mechanics/probability audits.

### Pharaoh's Gold

- Desktop and 320×680: the 3×3 board visibly contains the cobra, mask, ankh, scarab, blank position and Eye of Horus with a legible WILD label.
- Selecting one, two and three active lines updates the selected option and displayed active-line count without changing the total 1-credit stake.
- The rules dialog explicitly describes total-stake division, two-Cobra pays, the third-line Mask award, no free spins, provisional Ankh pay and unavailable live progressives.
- All essential controls fit in the mobile viewport. This pass checked selection and disclosure; exhaustive pay evaluation remains deterministic test coverage, not a claim that a random browser spin exercised every pay.

### Slide

- Added a second independently sized target using the real form. The tested slip was 1 credit at 2× and 1 credit at 5×, with a displayed total stake of 2 credits.
- Starting the round changed the wallet from 787.04 to 785.04 and disabled both target forms. Both wagers shared the single 1.07× stop. The result reported 0 of 2 wins, a 0.00 return, −2.00 net and separate lost results for both wagers.
- Controls re-enabled after settlement. The 320×680 form supports adding the second target and remains readable through normal vertical scrolling. Unlike the compact slot cabinets, this multi-target form is not intended to fit entirely above the fold.
- The page explicitly states local single-player play and no shared multiplayer server.

### Original Gates of Olympus

- Desktop and 320×680: inspected the full 6×5 painted-symbol board, cabinet and reachable stake/spin/tool controls.
- A 0.20-credit stake displays a 20.00-credit purchase, exactly 100×. The confirmation specifies 15 free spins at the locked stake and discloses local probability differences.
- A second purchase on mobile was observed at the actual entry frame with exactly four lightning-bolt cells and “4 scatters · Bonus symbols.” The original 15-spin starting award is also pinned in the engine fixtures; subsequent natural retriggers increased the displayed feature total.
- The first desktop purchase naturally retriggered twice by +5. Its displayed carried multiplier progressed from 12× to 15× and later 72×, persisted between spins, and disappeared when the feature completed.
- The completed first feature displayed a total return of 40.24. Its wallet reconciled as 785.04 − 20.00 + 40.24 = 805.28. Results are prepared and settled before presentation, as disclosed by the local architecture.
- Browser QA found a misleading “1 from bonus” label during a free-spin retrigger. This was corrected to use the configured base or free-spin threshold. Meeting the threshold now says “Bonus symbols” or “Retrigger symbols”; only engine award events announce a free-spin award. Six rendered-copy tests cover base, free, at/over threshold and alternate threshold cases. The corrected “4 scatters · Retrigger symbols” was then observed in the live mobile feature alongside the actual +5 event.
- The mobile purchase subsequently completed at a 3.60 total return, re-enabled controls, and reconciled the wallet as 805.28 − 20.00 + 3.60 = 788.88.

## Verification boundaries

- This final pass is targeted integration QA, not a new browser traversal of all 34 game routes. Earlier theme checks are recorded in [cabinet QA](art-v2/CABINET_QA_2026-10-08.md), while the final aggregate suite covers the complete checkout.
- Source-backed mechanics and local probability estimates are distinct. Private provider reel strips/PAR distributions, RTP identity and all operator-specific variants are not verified. See the [mechanics audit](MECHANICS_AUDIT_2026-10-08.md) and [slot probability audit](SLOT_PROBABILITY_AUDIT_2026-10-08.md).
- Pharaoh's non-center line placement, Ankh award and two-Cobra positional convention remain provisional; there is no live progressive pool. The operator's top-symbol prose and numerical table are not fully consistent.
- Wanted's exact numbered payline paths, Sweet's exact purchased-entry presentation, some Wolf/Bass money-value and reel correlations, and parity of every advanced provider control remain explicitly limited in the mechanics audit.
- Crash, Aviator and Slide do not implement shared hosted multiplayer, real external players, provider server hash chains or authentic hosted scheduling. Classic, Three Cups, Scratch Card and Bingo retain their disclosed local rules.
- Web Locks coordinate participating same-origin tabs, not different devices or profiles. Multi-key localStorage writes are not atomic database transactions. Earlier browser handover verification and the final lock regressions do not establish server-backed wallet guarantees. See [cross-tab play](CROSS_TAB_PLAY.md).
- Mobile testing here verifies responsive layout in Chromium, not native iOS Safari, Android hardware, touch latency, low-memory devices, every accessibility mode or every animation frame.

The work should be described as a materially improved and tested local play-money implementation with disclosed limits, not exact reproduction of every provider detail.
